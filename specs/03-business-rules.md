R1: A staff member cannot have overlapping active bookings, buffers included. Slots are half-open, so 9:00-10:00 and 10:00-11:00 don't overlap. Buffers never overlap: each booking blocks the staff member from its start minus its before-buffer to its end plus its after-buffer, and these blocked intervals cannot overlap. This assumes buffers model tasks the staff member performs (e.g. setup and cleanup), not minimum gaps between appointments; if buffers were gaps, overlapping them would be acceptable. A booking's buffers are fixed when it is made (or rescheduled); later changes to a service's buffers only affect new bookings (see [ADR 0004](../docs/adr/0004-snapshot-buffers-on-booking.md)).
R2: All times are stored in UTC, and the business’s IANA time zone is the reference for availability. Both the customer's and the business' time zones should be displayed. Skip spring-forward gap, offer slots for the first hour of a fall-back overlap. Bookings across a transition should list duration (actual elapsed time) and start and end times (in local time). For example, a 2-hour booking starting at 01:00 on a spring-forward night ends at 04:00 local. If a business changes its time zone, existing bookings keep their UTC times, and availability (weekly availability and date exceptions) keeps its local times, interpreted in the new zone. Bookings that have not started and are left outside availability are handled as an availability change under R6; if the owner does not confirm, the time zone is not changed. Customers whose remaining bookings that have not started now fall at a different local time in the business's time zone are notified of the new local times. The change takes the staff lock for every staff member in the business (see [ADR 0002](../docs/adr/0002-booking-concurrency-row-lock.md)), and each change is recorded with the old and new time zones, who made it and when.
R3: A booking cannot start within the business’s minimum notice period. The minimum notice period is a business setting with a default of 2 hours, which the owner can change. A change applies to slot generation and bookings made from then on; existing bookings are unaffected. The notice period also applies to the new slot when rescheduling. An owner or staff member may override the notice period for an individual booking or reschedule: staff only for their own bookings, owners for any booking in their business; the override bypasses only R3, so the booking must still not start in the past, must satisfy R1, and must start within the maximum advance-booking window (R8). Each override is recorded in the booking's history, including who made it.
R4: Customers may cancel up to N hours before the start, inclusive (cancelling exactly N hours before is allowed). After that, customers cannot cancel or reschedule (they must book a new slot instead); staff and owners still can. N is configured per business, with a default of 24 hours. If a booking is made for a customer with a business (by the customer, or by a staff member or owner on their behalf) while the customer holds a booking with that business that starts within the cancellation window, the new booking is made and the original is kept. Before confirming, the person making the booking is warned that the original booking still stands; a customer booking for themselves is also told they must contact the business to cancel it. Once the new booking is made, the original booking's staff member and the business's owner are notified that the customer holds both bookings, so they can cancel the original. See [ADR 0001](../docs/adr/0001-remove-late-cancellations.md) for why late cancellations were removed and why the original booking is not cancelled automatically.
R5: Rescheduling atomically moves a booking to a new slot; if the new slot is unavailable, the booking keeps its original slot. The booking keeps its ID, and each move is recorded in the booking's history (see [ADR 0003](../docs/adr/0003-reschedule-keeps-booking-id.md)). The move happens in one transaction under the staff lock described in [ADR 0002](../docs/adr/0002-booking-concurrency-row-lock.md).
R6: Time off that overlaps existing bookings requires confirmation. A booking overlaps time off if its blocked interval (R1, buffers included) overlaps it. The staff member or owner is warned and shown the overlapping bookings; if they confirm, the time off is saved, the overlapping bookings are cancelled, and cancellation notifications are sent to all affected users. If they do not confirm, nothing changes. Bookings cancelled this way are business-initiated, so the R4 cancellation window does not apply. Confirming takes the staff lock described in [ADR 0002](../docs/adr/0002-booking-concurrency-row-lock.md). A change to a staff member's availability (a date exception or a change to their weekly availability, see R8, or a change to the business's time zone, see R2) that leaves existing bookings outside that availability is handled the same way: warning, confirmation, cancellation and notifications, with the change saved only if confirmed. Removing a staff member who has Confirmed bookings that have not started is also handled this way; those bookings are cancelled with the reason StaffRemoved.
R7: Two simultaneous requests for overlapping time with the same staff member, buffers included (see R1), result in exactly one success and one clear conflict error. The conflict error is an HTTP 409 response whose body references the conflicting slot by staff member, start time and end time only; it never includes the other booking's ID or customer details. See [ADR 0002](../docs/adr/0002-booking-concurrency-row-lock.md) for how this is enforced.
R8: A staff member's availability on a date is their weekly availability, unless they have a date exception for that date, which replaces the weekly availability for that date entirely (different hours, or none). Slot generation never returns slots outside availability, in the past, past the maximum advance-booking window, or whose blocked interval (R1, buffers included) overlaps the staff member's time off. Availability bounds only the appointment itself, not its buffers: with availability from 09:00, a service with a 15-minute before-buffer is offered at 09:00. Each availability block (a weekly block or a date exception's range) bounds the appointment on its own, even when two blocks touch: with blocks 10:00-12:00 and 12:00-13:00, a 60-minute service is not offered at 11:30 (decided 2026-11-20; an owner who wants one continuous period enters one block). The same applies when deciding whether a change to availability leaves a booking outside it (R6). The maximum advance-booking window is configured per business, with a default of 3 weeks. Slot start times are spaced by a step that is configured per business, with a default of 30 minutes. Slots are end-exclusive intervals, so the advance-booking window is checked against the slot's start time; the window is inclusive (a slot starting exactly at the window's end is offered). Steps are counted from the start of each availability block (e.g. availability from 09:15 with a 30-minute step gives slots at 09:15, 09:45, …). A booking or reschedule must start at a time slot generation would offer for that staff member and service, so no one can book at an arbitrary time (e.g. 09:10); when the minimum notice is overridden (R3), slots inside the notice period count as offered.
R9: Reminders are scheduled 24 hours and 1 hour before a booking starts. A reminder whose time has already passed when the booking is made or rescheduled is skipped, not sent immediately. Rescheduling moves pending reminders to the new start (ADR 0003). Cancelling a booking, including a cancellation caused by time off (R6), deletes its pending reminders.
