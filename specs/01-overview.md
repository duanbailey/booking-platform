# Goals

Businesses publish services and availability, and customers book, reschedule, and cancel without conflicts, all through a web frontend ([ADR 0012](../docs/adr/0012-provide-web-frontend.md); screens in [09-ui.md](09-ui.md)).

# Non-goals

Payments, multi-location chains, calendar sync (Google/Outlook), native mobile apps

# Glossary

Business, Service, Staff, Availability Rule, Time Off, Booking, Slot.
